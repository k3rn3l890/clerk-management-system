Act as a senior full stack developer and create a comprehensive court clerk management system for a ghanaian court. I want you to build this system using this stack; HTML,CSS, javascript, bootstrap and php. I would like to connect it to an sql database using xammp too as well. 


These are the users and features I want to implement for the system:

A. Internal Court Users (Primarily Staff ):

1.  Court Clerks:
    Who: These are the primary users and would include various levels of clerks
    Why & What Access: They need comprehensive access to manage the lifecycle of cases.
    Functions: Initiating new cases, updating case information, managing electronic documents (uploading, viewing), scheduling hearings, sending out electronic notices, recording court fees/fines, generating simple reports on the go, internal messaging with judges or other staff.

2.  Judges:
    Who: Judicial officers presiding over cases.
    Why & What Access: To access case files, dockets, and manage their schedules efficiently.
    Functions: Viewing case files and documents, managing personal court calendars, secure communication with clerks.


3.  Judicial Secretary & Court Administrators/Managers:
    Who: Senior administrative staff responsible for the overall management and operational efficiency of the courts.
    Why & What Access: For oversight, performance monitoring, generating reports, and administrative functions.
    Functions: Viewing dashboards with court performance statistics, accessing reports, broadcasting important notices to court staff.

4.  IT Administrators / System Support Staff:
    Who: Technical staff responsible for maintaining the application.
    Why & What Access: Full system access for maintenance, troubleshooting, user management, and security monitoring.
    Functions: Monitoring system health, managing user accounts (e.g., password resets for urgent cases), receiving system alerts.





B. External Users (Interacting with the Court):

1.  Lawyers (Members of the Ghana Bar Association):
    Who: Legal practitioners representing clients.
    Why & What Access: To file documents, track their cases, and receive notifications.
    Functions: E-filing of documents, viewing case status and history for their specific cases, receiving court schedule notifications, accessing calendars for their cases, secure communication with the court registry on case-specific matters.


2. Litigants / Public Users (Citizens):
   Who: Individuals who are parties to a case, or the general public seeking information.
   Why & What Access: Limited, secure access to their own case information or general public information.
   Functions: Checking the status of their own case (with secure login), viewing hearing dates for their case.
